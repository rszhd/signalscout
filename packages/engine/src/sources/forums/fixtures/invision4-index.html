      In celebration of my 5th Folding Month coming up in a few days, I wanted to have 5 computes folding simultaneously for the first time. In the past, the most I ever did at one time was 4. However, I got given a laptop by my in-laws, and it actually has a dGPU. I felt like I had to include that. So, here's 6 computers all folding for Team LTT. [image] I doubt I'll keep these all going for the entire month, but they're chugging along right now. We'll see how the laptops fare. They might actually be worth it from a power efficiency standpoint even if from a raw points metric they are way weaker than the desktops. You can tell from the PPD numbers that the two K620 cards have the exact same GPU core as the 940MX does. I apparently have accidentally started a collection of GM107 silicon. The Quadros are actually very power efficient out of the box and I further limited them to just 20W each. I'll probably keep Ceres up through he whole month, as it's also my NAS, so it's on 24/7 anyway. How well Jupiter does this month will likely depend on how many HIP WU the RX 9060 XT is given. Right now, it's performing similarly to the RTX 2060 Super, which I've seen happen in past folding months for some WUs. Also, if you haven't registered yet, registration for the Folding Month is still open until 12pm UTC on Oct. 31st. [embed]
