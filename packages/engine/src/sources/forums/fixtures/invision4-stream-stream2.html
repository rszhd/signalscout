    I would lean towards the budget side and leave yourself an upgrade path. Start with a Ryzen 5 7500F, an Asrock B650M-HDV/M.2 or similar, and a 16GB stick of DDR5. You should be able to float that for under €350 pretty easily if you shop around. Add another stick of RAM when you have the funds and upgrade the CPU later.
