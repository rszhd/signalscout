      Not quite the smooth 4 hour drive today that I was hoping for. There's no direct route to Woodward in northwest Oklahoma from Wichita, so it was a series of zig-zags on state and US highways. The Honda got the worst of it. Arrived at the RV park and spent an hour giving both an impromptu bath. Not great, but I couldn't leave the mud caked on to completely dry. Not how I wanted to start my stay. Small RV park a couple miles southeast of town. Decent enough for two nights. The concrete pad is relatively level, and no tall trees. Had to get an early start today, so I didn't get a chance to book an RV park just east of Dallas for the 2nd week of November. Take care of that later. Going to take a short nap, and then head out for a bite to eat. I have a mid-morning round at Boiling Springs GC tomorrow. For some odd reason the first tee time isn't until 10 am. Apparently they're unaware that we set the clocks back an hour tonight, so sunrise will be at 7 am. Oy.

    7. Refurbished Wilson Signature Model - Gene Sarazen putter

      Refurbished Wilson Signature Model - Gene Sarazen putter

      member20 replied to member21's topic in Hickory, Persimmon and Classic Clubs
      I think you're confusing "Sam Snead" Signature with "Gene Sarazen" Strokemaster - they both show up in the 1952 catalog. Maltby's echoes that, also stating the "Gene Sarazen Strokemaster" irons are the same "old style" club head. Interestingly the shafts (in Maltby's, if ya'll trust that source) in '52 are Dynamic TT....changed in '53 & '54 to TT Step Down & after that to TT Rocket shafts (tho, the Kaplan catalog for those 2 years are TT Mecury shafts 😂) I have a Gene Sarazen Strokemaster 7 iron. I thought it was from the 60's (it's in very good condition). The stamping on the back does not match anything in Kaplan or Maltby of any year., the sole has a reg # 4102 , the shaft is a TT Step Down & the grip is a red leather with the flat reminder type. (Again, interestingly the reg# 4102 is on the Sam Snead Signature irons of that same year 🤣). The loft is ~40*. The thing about Kaplan's is that Wilson quit putting "Gateway to Golf/retail" in catalogs around 1962. Seems that Wilson, Hillerich & Bradsby, & MacGregor, all had retail lines not in all their catalogs sometime in the '60s or a bit later. So just cuz it isn't mentioned in the catalog, doesn't mean it wasn't made - and many of the pre 1970s retail clubs were the previous years professional models with different shafts, grips or just different colors. Willie Hoare didn't just design the "Winsum", he designed wood & iron sets back in the '30s (in '32, the first mentioned Hoare putter (a mallet) was described as persimmon with aluminium insert & metal sole offered w/a sheathed steel or hickory shaft). At the same time Gene Sarazen also had a putter that was hickory shafted.) So I'm guessing that while my 7 iron might be from '52/3. I'm thinking that the putter above might be a more '60s retail model....difficult to tell since the grip is not original and, if the person went to this complete refurbish, it wouldn't be difficult to guess the putter has been reshafted. Have fun....sounds like it putts well for you.
