FLAC cannot be bitstreamed, so Kodi is probably converting it to PCM stereo.

You could try setting the number of channels in the audio configuration to 2.0 (don't ask me why ) and then enable Dolby digital (AC3) transcoding under passthrough.

For PCM playback, you need to configure the amount of speakers in kodi's gui to the proper layout, while bitstream does not care about that setting. Kodi will decode multi-channel flac to float and output it as PCM format, but i will adjust to the configuration you gave it. Some device (AVR, etc.) cannot cope with multi channel PCM, for those the above mentioned AC3 Transcode path is the workaround.
